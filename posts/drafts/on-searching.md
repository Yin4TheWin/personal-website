**History didn't shatter with a scream; it unraveled with a sigh.**

It started with a single, sickening crunch. The rogue time traveller looked down, his coffee-deprived brain finally snapping awake. He had stepped off the predestined path, and beneath his boot lay a ruined butterfly.

You didn’t feel the reality-warping shockwave hit your bedroom. But when you woke up this morning, the world had fundamentally changed. The bottle of jam in your fridge became a delicious bottle of mealworms. Outside, your neighbors greeted each other with flickering tongues. And protruding from the back of your favorite trousers was a thick, scaly lizard tail.

The timeline had been corrupted. Somewhere between the dawn of January 1, 1900, and the midnight of December 31, 1999, reality fractured.
